About the Council

The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) was founded in 1958 to establish a high-quality educational framework across secondary learning environments. As an independent, authoritative board, CISCE continuously develops advanced curriculum plans, balanced assessment methods, and progressive grading scales that empower students nationwide.

Our academic framework is structured to verify conceptual mastery rather than rote learning. By keeping grading parameters uniform across all geographic zones, the Council preserves academic integrity for every student within our partner schools. Through systematic process reviews and continuous infrastructure optimization, CISCE has modernised its traditional methodologies to successfully match the stringent demands of higher education globally.

Foundational Mandate : To deliver highly precise, equitable evaluation systems designed to assess logical deduction, critical analysis, and practical reasoning proficiencies.

Curricular Architecture Philosophy

The standard CISCE curriculum is built upon deep conceptual training in natural sciences, mathematical computation, precise literacy, and socioeconomic studies. We emphasize practical application, laboratory experiments, and investigative field projects over purely abstract knowledge. This integrated model bridges theoretical frameworks with real-world problems, helping students cultivate critical thinking, adaptability, and analytical research skills early in their academic journey.

Institutional Scale and Quality Assurance

Connecting thousands of top-tier schools globally, the Council maintains strict verification workflows to ensure uniform grading conditions are followed everywhere. Continuous training initiatives for educators help our network deliver optimal academic standards across all divisions.

  • Aggregate Uniformity Mandate: The cumulative evaluation maximum for each of the thirteen registered subject disciplines stands strictly unalterable at an aggregate total of exactly 100 Marks, non-negotiable across all affiliated educational institutions.

  • Formative Internal Allocation Protocols: The designated 20 Marks component assigned to core and elective subjects must be distributed explicitly across continuous internal evaluations, certified laboratory journals, structured project portfolios, viva-voce interactions, or oral-aural performance metrics as strictly prescribed by the Council.
